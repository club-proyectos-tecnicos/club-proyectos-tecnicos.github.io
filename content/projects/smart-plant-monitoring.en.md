## Summary

**Sports trajectory prediction** is a project that studies movement in sports situations and builds a technical foundation for estimating trajectories from data.

It combines data analysis, predictive modelling and visualization to understand how a trajectory evolves and which variables may help anticipate its behavior.

The full report remains available as a downloadable document for readers who want to examine the methodology, development and conclusions in greater detail.

## Goal

The project's goal is to develop a technical approach for:

- representing sports trajectories in a structured form;
- analyzing relevant movement variables;
- preparing data for predictive models;
- evaluating results and limitations;
- documenting the process reproducibly.

## Approach

The project is conceived as a practical application of technical analysis to sports. Its central idea is to transform movement observations into data that can be studied, compared and used to build models.

The work follows a straightforward process: data preparation, exploration, modelling, validation and communication of results. This structure separates the experimental work from the final explanation and makes it easier to continue developing the project in future iterations.
