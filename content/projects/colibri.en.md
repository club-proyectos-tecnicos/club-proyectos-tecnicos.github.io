## Research question

A frequency-selective surface (FSS) is a periodic structure whose response depends on frequency. COLIBRI studies how changes in thickness, geometry and materials affect predicted electromagnetic absorption. The target frequency range is 2 to 18 GHz, with a single layer as the first reference case.

## Progress

The team has developed equivalent-circuit models, Python code and reproducible documentation for comparing a homogeneous layer with a circuit-analogue absorber. The diagram summarises the approach. Results to date are computational, with experimental validation still ahead.

## Why it matters

Documenting the assumptions, calculations and limits gives other students a way to reproduce the results, understand why the predictions change and review which conclusions the model supports.

## Next milestone

Assess sensitivity to thickness, surface resistance, angle and polarisation. Compare a reference case with a full-wave electromagnetic simulation before considering fabrication and measurement.
