## Design question

How could a drone hold interchangeable sensors for 3D mapping? CPTA explored a mechanical layout with space to integrate sensor modules.

## Work documented

CPTA produced an initial parametric CAD model (V0.2) and exported its geometry in September 2026. The model records the proposed arrangement. Assembly, flight and mapping remain unverified.
