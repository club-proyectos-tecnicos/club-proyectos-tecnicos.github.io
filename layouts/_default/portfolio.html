{{ $reforesta.Title }}

The decision

Reforesta is a nonprofit that restores woodland. Its field team has to spread its time and watering resources across several planting sites. Which areas should they inspect or water first?

{{ partial "reforesta-dashboard.html" $reforesta }}

Confirmed progress

We received an initial data sample on 29 October 2026 and are preparing a pilot limited to one area.

Next milestone

Agree on the pilot area with Reforesta. The proposed first deliverable is a private list of zones in priority order, with a reason for each ranking and a flag where information is too limited to decide with confidence.

How we would test it

Review the list with Reforesta’s field team and record which priorities they would change, why, and what information they are missing.

What we want to improve

An order the field team can check and explain could help them direct watering resources to the areas that need most attention. The pilot will test whether this makes planning easier.

How our teams work

We start with a decision or research question and agree on what the team can deliver. We build a first version, review it against the available evidence and document the assumptions and limits so that others can examine the work or take it forward.

A pilot who spends much of his working day in airports told us that queues and bottlenecks were a recurring problem. We set out to explore how airport staff could anticipate congestion and decide when to open more service desks.

{{ $aeroflow.Params.summary }} Earlier warnings could give staff time to move resources before queues build and could shorten passenger waits. The team tested the approach in a simulated terminal.

University support

CPTA is registered as a student club at Universidad CEU San Pablo through Campus Life. As of November 2026, CPTA reported around 40 members, with 20 assigned to project teams.

Proposed DSSD collaboration · January 2027

We propose starting our first project with Data Science for Sustainable Development (DSSD) in January 2027, subject to agreement with DSSD and a nonprofit partner.

We could draw on a group of five to eight CPTA students. We would agree with DSSD on the size of the core team based on the project scope and student availability. We would designate one CPTA project lead.

  1. Agree on the scope. The CPTA project lead and a partner contact would agree on one deliverable, data access and acceptance criteria.
  2. Review progress during the semester. The team would share an early version, meet regularly with the partner and record decisions and limitations.
  3. Hand over the result. The team would deliver the agreed tool or report with documentation, a walkthrough and an agreement on maintenance.
