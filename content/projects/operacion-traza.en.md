## The problem

A diffusion cloud chamber lets the audience observe the tracks left by ionising particles directly. The demonstration is designed to help people recognise the tracks and understand what causes them, even from the back of the classroom. The team plans to light the chamber so the tracks stand out, project a close-up for people farther away and record a backup video to explain the phenomenon if the live view is hard to see.

## Progress

The team has outlined a chamber, lighting and projection setup and written a test plan. The diagram illustrates that design. Assembly and trials to produce the team's own tracks are still ahead.

Universidad Complutense de Madrid lists its 15th exhibition of homemade experiments for 25 November 2026, from 11:00 to 12:30 Madrid time. This is the event window. [Faculty programme in Spanish](https://fisicas.ucm.es/difusion-de-la-investigacion).

## Next milestone

Observe a first track and check whether the result can be repeated. Then test visibility from across the room and record a backup video.
