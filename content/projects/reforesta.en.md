## The problem

The field team at [Reforesta](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/ceneam/recursos/quien-es-quien/reforesta.html) has to spread its time and watering resources across several planting sites. The decision is which areas to inspect or water first with the information available.

## Progress

The CPTA team received an initial data sample on 29 October 2026 and is preparing a pilot limited to one area. The first task is to agree on that area with Reforesta.

## Next milestone

Once the pilot area is agreed, the team would review what information is available and where it is incomplete. The proposed first deliverable is a private list of zones in priority order. Each entry would explain why it is ranked there and flag any gaps that make the decision uncertain.

## How we will test it

We would walk through the list with Reforesta's field team using their knowledge of the pilot area. For each priority they would change, we would record the change, the reason and what information they would need to make the call. We would use that feedback to see where the list supports planning and where it needs work.

## Why it matters

An order the field team can check and explain could help them direct watering resources to the areas that need most attention. The pilot will test whether the list makes that planning decision easier.
